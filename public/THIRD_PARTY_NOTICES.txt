SketchRNN Lab is an educational interface using pretrained SketchRNN inference.

Research: David Ha and Douglas Eck, A Neural Representation of Sketch Drawings (2017).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.03477

Original implementation and browser library: Google Magenta / The Magenta Team.

https://github.com/magenta/magenta

The original authors developed and trained the model. This app does not train it.
Model provenance, checksum, and licensing caveat: /model-source.json.
The cat, bird, and flower checkpoints are obtained separately by npm run prepare:model.
Quick, Draw! supplied the training sketches; the dataset and weights are distinct assets.
